Soul Winning Strategies

Practical guidance for sharing Christ confidently and effectively.

Before You Go Out

✓ Pray for God's guidance.
✓ Ask the Holy Spirit for boldness.
✓ Know your testimony.
✓ Carry a Bible or Gospel tract.
✓ Go with love and humility.


Starting Conversations

• Hello, may I share a message of hope with you?

• Do you attend a church anywhere?

• Can I pray for you today?

• If you died today, are you certain where you would spend eternity?

• Have you experienced God's love personally?


The Gospel in Simple Steps

1. God Loves Us
God created us and desires a relationship with us.


2. Sin Separates Us
Every person has sinned and fallen short of God's standard.


3. Jesus Christ Saves
Jesus died and rose again to provide forgiveness and eternal life.


4. Repent and Believe
We must turn from sin and place our faith in Christ.


5. Receive Christ
Salvation is a gift received by faith.

Salvation Prayer Guide

"Lord Jesus, I know that I am a sinner. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I repent of my sins and invite You into my life. Be my Lord and Savior. Thank You for saving me. Amen."


Follow-Up Strategy

Day 1 — Call and encourage.

Day 3 — Pray with them.

Day 7 — Invite them to church.

Week 2 — Introduce Bible study.

Month 1 — Connect them to a ministry group.


Common Challenges

Rejection
Not everyone will receive the message. Stay encouraged.


Difficult Questions
Be honest when you don't know an answer. Offer to follow up.


Fear
Remember that the Holy Spirit empowers believers to witness.


Lack of Results
Your responsibility is faithfulness. God gives the increase.


Evangelist's Commitment

I will pray faithfully.
I will share Christ boldly.
I will love people genuinely.
I will follow up intentionally.
I will trust God for the results.
